05 / Coaches

The view from the touchline

Coaches connect generations. They are remembered through methods, selection choices, trophies and the stories players repeat long after a season ends.

The strongest historical coaching image in the supplied folders is associated with Père Jégo, an important figure in Casablanca football history. The archive uses that image as the entrance to a broader coaching section rather than pretending the current collection already contains every coach.

A mature version of this section can be organized chronologically and linked to season pages. Each coach profile can then show the seasons, squads and major moments already stored elsewhere in the archive.

This is one of the places where written research matters most, because a photograph alone rarely explains tactical ideas or institutional influence. The visual record should invite research, not replace it.

Archive note: where an exact player, match or date is not documented by the supplied file itself, the page avoids pretending that identification is certain.
