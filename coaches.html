The view from the touchline

Coaches connect generations through selection, methods, trophies and stories that remain after a season ends.

The strongest historical coaching image in the supplied collection is associated with Père Jégo. It becomes the entrance to a broader coaching archive rather than pretending the current image set already contains every coach.

A mature coaching section should connect each profile to seasons, squads, major moments and verified research. The visual record invites that research; it does not replace it.
