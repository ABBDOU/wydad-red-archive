04 / Players

The shirt is carried by people

A club archive becomes human when the viewer can move from a team picture to a face, from a face to an era, and from an era to the stories supporters still tell.

The supplied collection includes several player-specific files, among them images labeled for Bencherifa, Talbi and Belkhouja. Those labels are useful archive metadata and are preserved here without inventing biographical detail that the files themselves do not prove.

Player pages should eventually combine photographs, verified career notes, positions, seasons and quotations. For now, the visual archive establishes the identity of the collection and creates places where that research can live.

The design treats portraits differently from match action: portraits are given room and neutral framing, while action images can use wider crops and stronger contrast.

Archive note: where an exact player, match or date is not documented by the supplied file itself, the page avoids pretending that identification is certain.
