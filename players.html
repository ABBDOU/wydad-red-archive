04 / Players

The shirt is carried by people

A club archive becomes human when visitors can move from a team photograph to a face, from a face to an era, and from an era to the stories supporters still tell.

The supplied collection includes player-specific files labeled for Bencherifa, Talbi and Belkhouja. Those labels are useful archive metadata and are preserved without inventing biographical detail the files themselves do not prove.

Portraits are given neutral framing and room to breathe. Action images can use wider crops and more contrast. This keeps visual treatment connected to the type of source rather than forcing every photograph into the same template.
