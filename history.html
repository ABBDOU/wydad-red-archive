01 / History

History is a chain of generations

The archive tells Wydad history through what survives: team portraits, places, lineups, trophy scenes and supporter memory.

Wydad was founded in Casablanca in 1937. A serious club archive therefore has to preserve more than results: it has to preserve how the club looked, where it played, and how each generation chose to be remembered.

The earliest material is modest in resolution. V4 treats those photographs as archival objects instead of stretching them into wallpaper. Their scale, contrast and imperfections remain part of the record.

By the 1950s, 1960s and 1970s, team portraits and annotated lineups reveal shirt design, posture, stadium context and the visual language of different eras. The 1992 material changes the emotional register, moving from formal documentation into continental celebration.

Where a player, match or exact date is not documented by the source file, the archive avoids presenting uncertain identification as fact.
