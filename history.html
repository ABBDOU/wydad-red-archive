01 / History

History is a chain of generations

The archive begins with photographs, not with a database. That changes how the story is told: a year is seen through shirts, faces, grounds, newspaper typography and the way a squad presents itself to the camera.

Wydad was founded in Casablanca in 1937, and the club’s history is inseparable from the social history of the city. A football archive therefore has to preserve more than results: it has to preserve visual evidence of how the club looked, where it played, and how each generation wanted to be remembered.

The earliest dated material in this collection belongs to the late 1930s and 1940s. These frames are modest in resolution, so this site deliberately presents them as archival objects rather than stretching them into modern full-screen imagery. The scratches, contrast and small dimensions are part of the record.

By the 1950s, 1960s and 1970s, the archive becomes richer in team portraits and annotated lineups. A single photograph can reveal a formation of names, a kit style, a stadium setting and the visual conventions of an era.

The 1992 material changes the emotional temperature. The collection contains both team imagery and trophy imagery, allowing the story to move from formal documentation into continental celebration. Later folders from the 2000s and 2010s add match action, training-ground scenes and supporter photographs.

Archive note: where an exact player, match or date is not documented by the supplied file itself, the page avoids pretending that identification is certain.
