06 / Trophies

Objects become collective memory

A cup is an object. The photograph of a cup being lifted records bodies, emotion and the instant achievement becomes shared memory.

The 1992 African material is the strongest trophy cluster in the supplied archive. It gives the site a natural long-read because the photographs move from team documentation to celebration.

Trophy pages work best when they combine three layers: verified competition history, surviving visual evidence and supporter memory.
