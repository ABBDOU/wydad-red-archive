07 / Places

Football memory has an address

Grounds, training centers and routes through Casablanca are part of the club’s history. They tell us where generations gathered before they tell us who won.

The supplied collection includes material associated with Stade Philip and the Benjelloun training ground. Those folders make “places” a genuine archive category rather than a decorative background.

Historical ground photographs remain contained at natural scale. Larger modern images can occupy more space because their source resolution supports it.
