07 / Places

Football memory has an address

Old grounds, training centers and routes through Casablanca are part of the club’s history. They tell us where generations gathered before they tell us who won.

The source collection includes material associated with Stade Philip and the Benjelloun training ground. Those folders make “places” a genuine archive category rather than a decorative background.

Historical ground photographs are shown as archival objects at their natural scale. Larger modern training-ground images can occupy more space because their source resolution supports it.

Over time this section can become a geographical layer of the archive, connecting photographs to neighborhoods, stadium names, training routines and supporter journeys.

Archive note: where an exact player, match or date is not documented by the supplied file itself, the page avoids pretending that identification is certain.
