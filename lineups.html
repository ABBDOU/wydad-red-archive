03 / Lineups

Eleven names can define an era

Lineups are where memory becomes specific. They connect faces to positions, players to generations and team photographs to the tactical language of their time.

The archive contains annotated historical material associated with 1966 and 1973 as well as modern team photographs. These records deserve a separate section because a lineup is more than a photograph: it is a map of relationships between players.

Where names are already printed on or clearly associated with an archival image, the site can preserve them. Where a photograph is unlabeled, the correct approach is to leave identification open until a reliable source confirms it.

Future lineup pages can add shirt numbers, positions, match context and coach information, but the photographic record remains the starting point.

Archive note: where an exact player, match or date is not documented by the supplied file itself, the page avoids pretending that identification is certain.
