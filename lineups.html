03 / Lineups

Eleven names can define an era

Lineups connect faces to positions, players to generations and team photographs to the tactical language of their time.

The archive contains annotated historical material associated with 1966 and 1973 alongside modern team photography. Those records deserve their own section because a lineup is not just an image: it is a map of relationships.

Where names are already printed on or securely associated with an archival image, the site can preserve them. Where a photograph is unlabeled, identification remains open until a reliable source confirms it.
