About / Method

A digital museum, not a content dump

The project organizes photographs, seasons, people, places and supporter culture into a connected archive while protecting the character of old sources.

01 / PRESERVE

Respect the source

Small historic scans are contained rather than stretched. Modern photographs can fill larger surfaces. We avoid fake detail.

02 / CONNECT

Build relationships

Stories connect to years, lineups to players, trophies to generations and stadiums to the geography of Wydad memory.

03 / EXPLORE

Use intelligence carefully

Search, recommendations, filters and recent-page memory help visitors discover related material without replacing historical judgement.
