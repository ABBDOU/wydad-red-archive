09 / Fans

The stands are part of the team

Supporters do not appear in this archive as background decoration. The crowd is its own historical subject: banners, color, density, celebration and the collective performance of belonging.

The 2011 folders contain some of the strongest high-resolution crowd and matchday photographs in the collection. They are used here at larger sizes because they can carry the page without artificial enlargement.

Fan culture changes the visual rhythm of the site. History pages are quieter and more museum-like; supporter pages are darker, more saturated and more kinetic. The content is still archival, but the emotional register is different.

A future oral-history layer could add supporter testimonies, chants, ticket scans and memories of specific nights. The site is already structured to hold those stories beside the photographs.

Archive note: where an exact player, match or date is not documented by the supplied file itself, the page avoids pretending that identification is certain.
