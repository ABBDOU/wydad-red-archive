09 / Fans

The stands are part of the team

Supporters are not background decoration. The crowd is its own historical subject: banners, routes, color, density, celebration and belonging.

The 2011 folders contain some of the strongest crowd and matchday photographs in the supplied collection. Those images can carry larger surfaces without artificial enlargement.

Fan culture changes the visual rhythm of the site. History pages are quieter and museum-like; supporter pages are darker, more saturated and more kinetic.
