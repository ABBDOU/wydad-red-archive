02 / Seasons

Campaigns are memory systems

A season is more than a final table: it is a squad, a shirt, a coach, a stadium route, a crowd and the photographs supporters keep.

The archive has clear modern clusters for 2003, 2004, 2005, 2007, 2009, 2010 and 2011. Those folders provide the chronological backbone of this section without pretending to contain a complete statistical database.

The 2010 and 2011 material is among the clearest photography in the collection, so those files can occupy larger surfaces. Older or smaller originals remain framed and contained.

Future season records can add verified fixtures, league position, cup runs, coaches and player appearances while keeping the photography as the visual primary source.
