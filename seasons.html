02 / Seasons

Campaigns are memory systems

A season is not only a final table. In an archive it is a set of recurring visual clues: the squad, the shirt, the coach, the home ground, the away trip, the crowd and the photographs people keep afterward.

The source folders give the modern section of the archive a useful chronological backbone: 2003, 2004, 2005, 2007, 2009, 2010 and 2011 all appear as distinct clusters. Rather than inventing complete match data, this page uses those folders as documented season collections.

The 2010 and 2011 photographs are among the clearest source files in the collection. They are therefore used at larger sizes, while older or smaller images are kept in tighter frames. This avoids the blurred “wallpaper” effect of the first live build.

As the archive grows, each season can be expanded with verified fixtures, league position, cup runs, coaches and player appearances. The site structure is designed so that those facts can be added without changing the visual system.

Archive note: where an exact player, match or date is not documented by the supplied file itself, the page avoids pretending that identification is certain.
